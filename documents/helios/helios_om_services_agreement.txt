OPERATIONS AND MAINTENANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT
(Excerpt — Commercial Terms Summary)

CONFIDENTIAL — CONTAINS COMMERCIALLY SENSITIVE PRICING TERMS
Disclosure restricted to parties and their counsel under Section 14.2

This Operations and Maintenance Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of July 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between HELIOS RENEWABLES LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Owner"), and TALON GRID SERVICES INC., a Texas corporation ("Service Provider").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Service Provider shall perform all scheduled maintenance, corrective maintenance, high-voltage switching, vegetation management, module washing, and 24/7 remote monitoring services for the Facilities listed in Exhibit A (aggregate nameplate capacity of 610 MW across six sites in Texas and New Mexico).

2. TERM
Initial term of five (5) years from the Effective Date, expiring June 30, 2030, with automatic one-year renewals unless either party provides notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days prior to expiration. Owner may terminate for convenience upon ninety (90) days' notice subject to the termination fee in Section 11.3.

3. COMPENSATION
3.1 Fixed Fee: $6,150 per MW per year, escalated annually at CPI plus 1.0%, invoiced monthly in arrears.
3.2 Corrective maintenance labor beyond 400 hours per site per year shall be billed at $118/hour (standard) and $177/hour (overtime/emergency).
3.3 Spare parts procured by Service Provider are billed at cost plus twelve percent (12%). Owner-furnished major equipment (transformers, inverter modules) remains Owner's procurement responsibility.

4. PERFORMANCE GUARANTEE
4.1 Service Provider guarantees fleet-wide Facility availability of ninety-six percent (96%) measured annually per site.
4.2 Liquidated damages of $85,000 per site per percentage point (or pro rata portion) below the guaranteed availability, capped at fifteen percent (15%) of the annual Fixed Fee for the affected site.
4.3 Availability calculation excludes force majeure events, grid curtailment, Owner-directed outages, and delays attributable to Owner-furnished equipment lead times.

5. KEY PERSONNEL
Service Provider shall maintain a dedicated site supervisor for each Facility and shall not reassign more than two site supervisors in any twelve-month period without Owner's written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

11.3 TERMINATION FEE
In the event of Owner's termination for convenience, Owner shall pay an amount equal to fifty percent (50%) of the remaining Fixed Fees for the balance of the then-current term, not to exceed $9.4M.

14.2 CONFIDENTIALITY
The pricing, rate schedules, and liquidated damages terms of this Agreement constitute trade secrets of both parties. Neither party shall disclose such terms except to affiliates, lenders (including Crescent Bank as Administrative Agent), and advisors bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than this Section.
