Helios Renewables Celebrates One-Year Anniversary of Sun Mesa Phase I; Phase II Expansion On Track for Mid-2026

AUSTIN, TX — October 28, 2025 — Helios Renewables, a leading independent developer and operator of utility-scale solar and storage assets across Texas and the Southwest, today marked the one-year anniversary of commercial operations at Sun Mesa Phase I, its flagship 180 MW solar facility in Pecos County, Texas. Phase I achieved commercial operation on November 3, 2024 and has delivered over 420 GWh of clean energy in its first year, enough to power approximately 78,000 homes.

"Sun Mesa is the cornerstone of our growth strategy, and its first year has validated everything we believed about this site," said Dana Okafor, Chief Executive Officer of Helios Renewables. "With Phase II, we will more than double the project's capacity and cement Sun Mesa's place among the largest solar complexes in West Texas."

Sun Mesa Phase II, a 220 MW expansion currently under construction with EPC partner Talon Grid Services, remains on schedule for commissioning in the second quarter of 2026. Upon completion, the combined 400 MW complex will generate enough electricity to power more than 170,000 homes annually.

The company also highlighted its long-term power purchase agreement with Ridgeline Utilities, signed March 12, 2024, which anchors the project's contracted revenue through 2039.

"Our partnership with Talon Grid Services and the support of our lending group led by Crescent Bank have been instrumental in keeping this project moving forward," said Okafor.

Helios Renewables operates a portfolio of 610 MW across six sites in Texas and New Mexico, with a development pipeline exceeding 1.2 GW.

About Helios Renewables

Helios Renewables LLC is an independent power producer focused on utility-scale solar and battery storage in ERCOT and WECC markets. Headquartered in Austin, Texas, the company develops, owns, and operates renewable generation assets serving utility and corporate offtakers.
